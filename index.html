Engineering Expertise & Methodology

Structured Delivery From Site Diagnostics to Verified Handover

A disciplined five-phase engineering timeline keeps structural decisions documented, schedules controlled via MS Project, and live client sites protected throughout execution.

Client & Consultant Visibility Protocol 4 Governance Controls
01 Regular Communication Daily & weekly technical updates keep stakeholders aligned
02 Recorded Decisions Site anomalies resolved promptly with BUET/CAAB consultants
03 Verified Billing Every payment reconciled against measurement-book audits
04 Early Risk Resolution Soil and existing-rebar variances addressed before delay
  1. 01
    Phase 01 • Diagnostics CPT/SCPT & NDT Audit

    Understand the Brief & Site

    Align on project needs, structural and sub-soil diagnostics (CPT/SCPT, NDT, core testing), site constraints, and real conditions on the ground before quoting or mobilizing.

  2. 02
    Phase 02 • Planning BOQ & MS Project Schedule

    Plan Scope, Budget & Sequence

    Establish detailed BOQ, rate analysis, CAPEX/OPEX budget, phased work sequencing for live facilities, and MS Project programme prior to mobilization.

  3. 03
    Phase 03 • Coordination BUET / CAAB / Statutory Alignment

    Coordinate Design, Approvals & Procurement

    Bridge architects, structural consultants (BUET/CAAB), statutory compliance (BNBC/RAJUK/FSCD/DoE), and vetted material procurement.

  4. 04
    Phase 04 • Execution & QA/QC Stage-Gate QA/QC & HSE

    Build, Inspect & Communicate Progress

    Enforce rebar and concrete QA/QC, NDT audits, site safety toolbox talks, and transparent daily/weekly technical progress reporting.

  5. 05
    Phase 05 • Handover Measurement-Book Closeout

    Complete Handover & Closeout

    Verify finishing quality, reconcile measurement books and final bills against verified work, and deliver clean closeout documentation.
